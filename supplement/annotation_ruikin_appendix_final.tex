\section*{Supplementary note on auxiliary annotation and Ruikin}

The auxiliary annotation layer consists of anonymous questionnaire responses to displayed synthetic-session summaries. It is used only as a plausibility check for displayed-summary interpretability. It is not part of the main simulation evidence, does not estimate inter-rater reliability because there was no repeated session overlap, and does not establish external construct validity.

Ruikin is retained as a supplementary annotation-calibrated diagnostic layer. Detailed model-family comparisons, negative controls, direct-matched checks, and questionnaire materials are supplementary only. The main paper does not depend on Ruikin; its main claims are supported by the simulation protocol, verification checks, risk-transfer experiment, component diagnostics, and robustness summaries.
