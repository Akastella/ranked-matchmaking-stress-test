# Annotation Codebook

## perceived_unfairness_1_7

How unfair the session appears from the player-facing sequence. Use higher values for repeated large skill gaps, one-sided expected outcomes, or repeated exposure to smurf-like mismatch.

## frustration_risk_1_7

How likely the session seems to create adverse player-facing pressure. This is a judgment item, not a claim about real churn or satisfaction.

## progression_mismatch_salience_1_7

How salient the mismatch between visible rank movement and hidden estimate appears.

## session_judgment

Allowed values:

- acceptable
- concerning
- unsure

## optional_comment

Use this for short qualitative notes about why a session was difficult to rate.
