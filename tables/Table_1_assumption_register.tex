Team formation & 5v5 teams use role and MMR-balance objectives. & Simplifies synergy and communication. \\
Waiting pressure & Bounded proxy derived from queue and wait settings. & Not calibrated to real seconds. \\
Visible rank & Rank points move more slowly than hidden MMR. & Not a commercial rank formula. \\
Exposure debt & Equal-weight index over normalized exposure channels. & Not an absolute risk metric. \\
Rank--MMR hysteresis & Area between aligned hidden MMR and visible rank over time. & Lag is diagnostic, not automatically harmful. \\
